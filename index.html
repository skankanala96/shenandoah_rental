Your Foxhole Escape

A cozy log cabin where every detail invites you to slow down.

Pet-friendly log cabin in the heart of the Shenandoah Valley — perfect for relaxing getaways or adventure-filled weekends. Enjoy a private hot tub under the stars, game room, and multiple cozy living spaces for families and groups.

New, fully furnished with 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and 3 finished levels of thoughtfully designed space — from the loft lounge with daybed to the lower-level game area, with heated floors throughout the main floor and basement, plus a rain-head walk-in shower in the primary suite.
